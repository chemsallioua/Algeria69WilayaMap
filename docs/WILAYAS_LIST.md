# Complete List of Wilayas

Algeria has 69 wilayas. Codes 01–48 date from the 1974 and 1984 reorganisations, 49–58 were created by Law 19-12 (2019), and 59–69 by Law 26-06 of 4 April 2026, numbered by Presidential Decree 26-206.

"Carved from" is the wilaya each newer wilaya was split off. The same data is in [`data/wilayas.csv`](../data/wilayas.csv).
