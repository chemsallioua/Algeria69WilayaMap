In practice:

- **Credit**: when you publish a map or app that uses these boundaries, show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" (see [openstreetmap.org/copyright](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)).
- **Share-alike**: if you distribute modified boundary data (for example, edited outlines in a new GeoJSON), it must remain under the ODbL. Maps, images and apps that only display the data can use any licence.

Everything else in this repository, including the names and codes in `data/wilayas.csv`, the build script and the documentation, is under the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
